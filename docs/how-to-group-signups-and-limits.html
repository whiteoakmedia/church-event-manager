Church Event Manager|Setting up a retreat sign-up

One person, a whole family.

Here's how to set up a sign-up where someone registers herself and her daughters, and each of them picks their own workshop.

  1. Open your event. Under Capacity & deadlines, set Guests per sign-up to 6.

    This is what lets one person sign up more than themselves.

  2. Go down to Registration Form Fields and click Add Field.

  3. Label it Workshop. For the type, choose Multiple Choice.

  4. Click Add option for each workshop, and put how many people it holds in the Limit box next to it.

    Leave a limit empty if that one has no cap.

  5. Tick Required, then tick Per person.

    "Per person" is the important one. It asks each family member separately instead of once for the whole booking.

  6. For the under-21 question, add a second field the same way: label it Age group, add the options 21 and over and Under 21, and tick Per person.

  7. Under Confirmation email, type your payment instructions. Paste your giving page address straight in and it becomes a link in the email.

  8. Update the event, then try it yourself: open the sign-up page, set the number of attendees to 3, and check you're asked for a workshop and an age group for each person.

A few things worth knowing

A workshop that reaches its limit shows Full and can't be chosen. Everyone else sees how many spots are left.

To see who chose what, go to Registrations and click a name. You'll see each person on that booking with their answers.

Answers can now be un-picked. There's a Clear selection link under any optional question, so nobody gets stuck with a wrong tap.

One person can sign up 25 people at most. Beyond that they'd register the rest separately.
